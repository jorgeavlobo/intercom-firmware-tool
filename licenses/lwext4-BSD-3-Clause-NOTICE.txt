lwext4 — copyright notices for the vendored (compiled-in) source
================================================================

`SharpExt4.dll` statically links the lwext4 library
(https://github.com/gkostka/lwext4). Because two modules
(`lwext4/src/ext4_xattr.c` and `lwext4/src/ext4_extent.c`) are GPL-2.0, the library
as a whole is distributed under the GNU GPL v2 (see `lwext4-LICENSE.txt`). The remaining modules
are under permissive licenses — predominantly the 3-clause BSD license reproduced
below, with a few imported files under other permissive terms. Their copyright
notices are still required to accompany binary redistributions and are reproduced
here.

The **authoritative, verbatim per-file** copyright and license statements ship with
the binary in the pinned corresponding-source archive
(`third_party/SharpExt4/SharpExt4-359d5f4.zip`, under `lwext4/`); this file is the
aggregated summary.
