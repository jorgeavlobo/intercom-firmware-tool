Notes on license election
-------------------------
* Many components are offered under a choice of licenses (for example
  "MIT OR Apache-2.0"). For every such component we elect the MIT License
  where MIT is one of the offered options, and Apache-2.0 otherwise. Only the
  terms of the elected license need be honored for that component, but because
  the binary as a whole aggregates all of the components, the complete text of
  every distinct elected license is reproduced once in the "FULL LICENSE TEXTS"
  section below.

  and are NOT dual-licensed away:
